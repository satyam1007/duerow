Duro Colors Private Limited

Manufacturing premium coatings since 2007 with a commitment to quality and innovation.

Established in 2007, we manufacture high-quality paints, primers, putties, tile grouts, adhesives, and epoxy coatings. Our commitment to excellence has made us a trusted name in the Indian paint industry.

Since April 25, 2018, we've partnered with Regent Paints Inc., USA, gaining access to advanced formulations and technical expertise to deliver products meeting global standards.

Under this partnership, we are licensed to repackage and relabel Regent Paints products for distribution across India, ensuring that our customers receive international-grade solutions tailored to local needs.

Our mission is to deliver high-performance, eco-friendly coatings while maintaining the highest quality standards. We believe in continuous research and development to bring cutting-edge technology to our customers.

At Duro Colors, customer satisfaction is our top priority. Our dedicated support team ensures smooth and timely delivery, expert consultation, and dependable after-sales service.
